Moves

    How to play
    • Drag tiles from your rack onto the board, or tap a tile and then a square.
    • Or click a square and type. Click it again to switch between across and down. Backspace takes back a letter.
    • Tap a tile you’ve placed to take it back. Drag tiles along the rack to rearrange them.
    • The first word covers the centre star. After that, every word joins onto tiles already down.
    • TW triples a word, DW doubles it, TL and DL triple or double a letter. Using all seven tiles scores 50 extra.
    • A blank can be any letter and scores nothing.
    • Enter plays and Esc takes your tiles back.
